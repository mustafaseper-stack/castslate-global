Four Core Pillars of CastSlate

Everything you need to deliver flawless lectures, perfectly integrated.

📺

1. Local Screen Broadcasting

Mirror your device's screen to any smartboard or computer browser on the same Wi-Fi network instantly. No cables, no lag, and no complicated setup. Just scan the QR code and your screen is live in the classroom.

📱

2. Teacher Remote Control

Untether yourself from the smartboard. Use your device as a wireless touchpad, instantly snap and send textbook questions to the board, type text remotely, or use your phone as a gyro-laser pointer to highlight content.

✍️

3. Interactive Whiteboard

A powerful canvas designed for educators. Features palm rejection (Stylus-only mode), smart geometry tools (compass, ruler, protractor), auto-shape recognition, vector erasing, and a built-in KaTeX math engine for complex formulas.

☁️

4. Cloud Teaching Module BETA

Take your classroom online. Generate a secure 5-digit PIN and stream your live whiteboard sessions over the internet. Students anywhere in the world can join from their browsers and follow your lecture in real-time with zero latency.
